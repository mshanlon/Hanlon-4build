About The Artist

Andrew Hozier-Byrne, known professionally as Hozier, is an Irish singer and songwriter known for blending blues, soul, and folk rock. His music often explores themes of love, religion, activism, and human rights.

Popular Albums

Hozier (2014)

Hozier's self-titled debut album includes the hit that launched his career “Take Me to Church,” which explores themes of identity and religion.

Wasteland, Baby! (2019)

The sophomore apocalypse album explores the imagery of the end of the world and the rarity and preciousness of love.

Unreal Unearth (2023)

In the wake of a pandemic riddled world comes this album inspired by Dante's Inferno, which takes the listener through emotional, spiritual depths, and the layers of hell.

Themes

  • Love and devotion
  • Critique of religion and exploration of religious trauma
  • Human rights, equality, and justice
  • Nature, land, and mortality
